## 1. Market Size & Opportunity

The short-form video editing software market is exploding:
- **Global video editing software market:** $3.1B (2024) → projected $6.9B by 2030 (CAGR ~13%)
- **Creator economy:** 200M+ content creators globally, 50M+ "professional" creators
- **Demand driver:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts — all require vertical video, fast turnaround
- **Pain point:** Raw iPhone footage → polished Reels takes 2-4 hours manually; creators want it in minutes

**TAM/SAM/SOM:**

|---|---|---|---|
| TAM | 50M content creators | $144/yr | $7.2B |

## 2. Competitive Landscape

### Direct Competitors

| Product | Pricing | Key Features | Weakness |
|---------|---------|--------------|---------|
| **Opus Clip** | $19/mo+ | AI clip extraction, reframing | No custom edit, limited control |
| **CapCut** | Free/$10 | All-in-one, TikTok native | Owned by ByteDance (trust issues), not AI-first |
| **Descript** | $24/mo+ | Transcript-based editing | Complex, learning curve, desktop-heavy |
| **Submagic** | $20/mo | Auto-captions, B-roll | Limited to subtitles, basic |
| **Munch** | $49/mo+ | Repurpose long→short | Expensive, limited customization |

### VibeMotion's Differentiation
1. **Full pipeline from raw iPhone → polished video** (not just one feature)
2. **AI understands WHAT is shown** (Gemini Vision) — not just audio transcription
3. **Smart A-roll/B-roll classification** — automatic visual storytelling
4. **Open, developer-friendly** — Remotion-based, composable
5. **Price competitive** — $12/mo vs $20-49/mo for competitors

## 3. Target Customer

### Primary: The "Vlogger Pro"
- Age 18-28, posts 3-5x/week on TikTok/Reels
- Has a day job or student, can't afford 4hrs/video
- Spends $20-50/mo on content tools already
- Pain: "I have raw footage but no time to edit"
- **WTP:** $12-20/mo for automation

### Secondary: The "Business Creator"
- Small business owner, marketer, solo consultant
- Creates content for lead generation
- Values professional output over custom style
- **WTP:** $20-49/mo for reliability

### Tertiary: The "Developer Creator"
- Like Sammy himself — technical + creative
- Wants API access, Remotion customization
- **WTP:** $49-99/mo for pro tier

## 4. Pricing Strategy

### Recommended Model: Freemium → Pro

| Tier | Price | Limits | Rationale |
|------|-------|--------|-----------|
| **Free** | $0 | 3 exports/mo, 720p | Low barrier to try; show value |
| **Pro** | $12/mo | Unlimited exports, 1080p60 | Under all competitors, impulse-buyable |
| **Team** | $39/mo | 5 seats, 4K, API access | Future; don't build yet |

**Why $12?**
- Under Opus Clip ($19), under Descript ($24), under CapCut Pro ($10 but inferior)
- "Obviously worth it" price — 1 saved hour of editing = worth way more than $12
- Low enough to expense on personal cards, high enough to be sustainable

## 7. Key Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| CapCut copies AI features | High | Speed to market, community moat |
| API costs spiral | Medium | Usage limits, rate limiting, cost monitoring |
| Render quality inconsistent | Medium | QA pipeline, user feedback loop |

| Remotion licensing issues | Low | Check license: MIT for basic use |

## 8. Moat & Defensibility

1. **Data flywheel:** Every video processed teaches the AI what works
2. **Creator community:** Discord, templates, presets = switching cost
3. **Sammy's brand:** 11K LinkedIn followers = personal distribution built-in
4. **Open + customizable:** Developers extend it; CapCut can't
5. **Price point:** Hard to undercut $12 profitably unless VC-funded
